Template library

Eight follow-up templates. The call picks one by what actually happened on it. Each is a file you can edit.

The router reads them in this order and stops at the first one that fires. When none fire, the call keeps its plain follow-up email.

  • fixed textShips word for word from the file. The model never rewrites it.
  • filled from backed notesFilled from claims the gate checked, each carrying its claim id.
  • instruction to the modelHouse rules for the draft. The buyer never sees this line.
1 No next step re-engagement no-next-step-reengagement A call happened and nothing was agreed. The honest case. Fires when the call has a summary, and either nothing was agreed as a next step, or no next step was found. It stays quiet if a next step was agreed with a date, something to send, or a loose check back. Open the anatomy

On the call page it says: No next step was agreed, so this template picked itself. A free model drafted it. The gate checked every line.

7 lines: 4 fixed text, 2 filled from backed notes, and 1 instruction to the model.

  1. 1 fixed text greeting Hi {{recipient}},
  2. 2 fixed text opener Thanks for the time today. Here is what I took away.
  3. 3 filled from backed notes What we coveredsummary What the call was actually about.
  4. 4 filled from backed notes What we coveredpain Anything new the buyer named as a problem. Renders nothing when there was none.
  5. 5 instruction to the model Rewrite the lines above as one short email in the second person. Every sentence that says something about the call must carry the claim id it came from. We did not land on a next step, so ask one open question about what would be useful. Never invent a step, a date, or a reason to hurry. Short sentences.
  6. 6 fixed text cta We did not land on a specific next step. What would be useful to you right now?
  7. 7 fixed text signoff Best, {{sender}}
